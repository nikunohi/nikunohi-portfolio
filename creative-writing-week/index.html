CREATIVE WRITING WEEK

Promotional materials designed for the SJSU English department’s Creative Writing Week 2024 event, including a digital poster, social media post series, and email header.

The digital poster was displayed on the client’s webpage. The design features modern internet and digital elements such as a mouse pointer, file thumbnail, and transparency grid. This representation of creative writing was chosen to address the shift of writing from a handwritten to a typed medium. The flat corner of the aforementioned file thumbnail is a motif throughout the branding, used to cap the corners of bodies of text. The poster became the basis for the stylization of the rest of the campaign.

The Instagram posts were designed to reflect the significance of a major, multi-day event. The graphic takes up the top six posts of the Instagram profile wall and provides a timeline of the week’s events.

For the email header, content from the poster is stripped down to the most crucial information, and design elements are simplified and scaled up to effectively fit the limited dimensions and size of the header.
