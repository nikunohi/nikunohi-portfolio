About

Sunshine City is an orihon (折本) style folding booklet which illustrates a narrative based on several interviews with a subject.

One of the attitudes that I took note of during the interview was the contrast of my client’s preferences between urban and natural environments. For example, they like rollerblading (an activity that is almost exclusively limited to manmade pavement), but simultaneously expresses a desire to escape to nature. Later on in our interview, they expressed that the fear and unknown evoked by nature are exactly why they feel so drawn to it.

I capture these contradictions and conflict, as well as follow their fleeing steps into nature, through the aforementioned poem. These words are collaged across the panels with warped text. The text becomes more warped the further right we go, mimicking the breakdown of systems and order as civilization transitions to wilderness. This transition is also marked by the changing complexity and density of the photographs collaged, as well as their subject matter. The left side is much more densely packed with sharp-edged vectors and photos of manmade structures. The right side has more organic shapes and naturalistic imagery. This gradual shift captures the contradicting extremes of my client’s identity and presents it in a dynamic collage.
