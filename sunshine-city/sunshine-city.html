SUNSHINE CITY

Sunshine City is an orihon (折本) style 8-panel pamphlet which illustrates a story based on several interviews with a subject.

The interviews were conducted using a predetermined list of open-ended questions covering a wide range of topics.

The piece portrays the subject: fascinated with the act of escaping, possibly unprepared for the implications of absolute freedom.

One of the attitudes that I took note of during the interview was the seemingly paradoxical tendency of the subject’s preferences towards urban and natural environments. For example, they enjoyed rollerblading —an activity that is almost exclusively limited to manmade pavement— but simultaneously expressed a desire to escape into nature completely. Later on in our interview, they expressed feeling drawn to the wilderness by the fear and unknown evoked by nature.

I interpret these contractions by capturing the delicate balance between folly and freedom intrinsic to the subject's escape. A poem narrating the liberation is collaged across the panels with warped text. The text becomes more warped the further right we go, mimicking the breakdown of systems and order as civilization transitions to wilderness.

This transition is further marked by the changing complexity and density of the photographs collaged, as well as their subject matter. The left side is more densely packed with sharp-edged vectors and photos of manmade structures. The right side has more organic shapes and naturalistic imagery.
