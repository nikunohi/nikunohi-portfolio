About

During my junior year in college I joined the BAD Design Club—a space for SJSU students to socialize and participate in design workshops—as a member of their council.

We were considering putting up physical posters promoting each event, as we were struggling to find other avenues to promote our club aside from social media posts, but with meetings every other week and only a small design team of 2 full-time students, creating physical and digital promotional material along with meeting recap visuals on such a short turnaround time was a challenge.

After sitting on the problem for a bit, I came across this site while completing work for a class. The idea of having a single-page website that existed only to fulfill a very specific goal stuck with me. Why not create a single-page website with the sole objective of spreading the word on the upcoming BADS event? This way, we could link to it using a permanent physical QR code poster that didn’t require us to redesign the physical posters from scratch every other week. By making the site a full-scroll, we could also just keep adding the latest events at the top of the page while displaying the promotions for past events in a cascading list underneath. The resulting site not only allowed us to promote upcoming events, but gave first-timers an idea of what past activities we had hosted.
