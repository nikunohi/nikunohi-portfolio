NEVER-ENDING MEETINGS

A promotional campaign for the BAD Design Club including: QR-code poster & sticker, webpage with information on upcoming club events.

In order to keep a small graphic design team from having to redesign physical flyers for events taking place on short turnaround, I chose to design a QR code poster that links to a webpage with up-to-date information on upcoming club events.

The site is a full-scroll, with information for past events displayed in a cascading chronological list underneath the latest event. The resulting site not only allows for promotion of upcoming events, but gives those unfamiliar an idea of what past workshops were like. The website was designed with longevity in mind, and can be reused with successive physical posters as long as the QR code is included.

The physical poster mirrors the spirit of the full-scroll webpage by being fully vertically tileable. QR codes cropped off at the edge of one page will seamlessly align to the next. Posters can be stitched together to create a visually striking, vertically disproportionate column to slap proudly on the campus walls.

The evergrowing verticality of both digital and physical aspects of this campaign reflects the capacity for the BAD Design Club to exist in perpetuity, abiding by a simple yet eternal set of rules (listed on the webpage). So long as workshops continue to be hosted, the webpage will continue to grow. Accordingly, I chose to name this campaign “Never Ending Meetings”.
