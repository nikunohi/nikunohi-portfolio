About

Stand Out! is a booklet of four spreads—all sharing a grid—highlighting techniques and elements of image making.

The image-forward booklet takes influence from children’s educational picture books with succinct and minimal text with large, inviting visual aids. Each spread title is treated specifically to correspond with the subject covered, providing an example of how type and image often overlap in their purpose to illustrate information.

The spreads themselves serve as a case study for image-making within a visual system, as all spreads are separated by content, but share an unorthodox grid system, and a visual style, that roughly informs each composition.
