STAND OUT!

Stand Out! is a booklet investigating elements of image making. Spreads are united with the use of an experimental grid.

The image-forward booklet takes influence from children’s educational picture books with minimal, succinct text and large, inviting visuals. Each spread title is treated specifically to correspond with the subject covered, providing an example of how type and image often overlap in their purpose to illustrate information.

The spreads also serve as a study for image-making within a visual system, as all spreads are separated by content, but brought together by an unorthodox grid system and visual style that informs each composition.
