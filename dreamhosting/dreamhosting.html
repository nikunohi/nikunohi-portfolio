DREAMHOSTING

Dreamhosting is a motion graphic depicting an online service that lives in the dreams of a small dog.

The looping motion graphic constantly moves between faux-analog and experimental textures in order to evoke the nebulous and bizarre visuals I often experience when dreaming.

Many of the style choices also reflect old-web and early digital visuals that I remember seeing throughout my childhood. The sound design is kept minimal to evoke a feeling of isolation and relative silence that I associate with a world only beginning to integrate itself into an interconnected network.
